Warp Pulse — Face the void

Face the void.

Link the orbs and launch your tethers to knock others away, trigger splits, and collect power-ups. A fast-paced arcade game built for multitouch, Warp Pulse rewards quick reactions and sharp eyes. The controls are simple, but lasting longer and winning takes strategy. Fight the pull of the central black hole and stay in the game as long as you can. Every move can change the course of a run.

Two modes. One challenge.

Can you be the best?

Can you outlast the competition? In Survival, beat your record and climb the leaderboard. In Stages, complete as many stages as you can and aim for the top. Only the top 1,000 players make the rankings.

01 / SURVIVAL

Last as long as you can.

Knock orbs back, collect power-ups, and fight the pull of the central black hole. Every second counts toward your record and the leaderboard.

02 / STAGES

Get through as many stages as you can.

Complete stage objectives that grow longer as you progress. Go as far as you can and try to take the lead on the leaderboard.

TETHERS & POWER-UPS

Link. Aim. Launch.

Link two orbs with a tether, then launch it to knock others away. Trigger splits and collect power-ups to keep your run going.

Survival power-ups

Every move counts.

Three effects to control the chaos. Use them at the right moment: the central black hole never slows down.

PURPLE / SLOW FIELD

Slow field

Orbs inside the field move much more slowly toward the center. Only one slow field can be active at a time.

YELLOW / MAGNET

Magnetic field

Orbs are pulled together at the center of the field. When it fades, they fly off in every direction. Only one magnetic field can be active at a time.

BLUE / RARER

Create more orbs

This rarer power-up adds new orbs to the board when you start running low.

GRAVITY
Bottom of the screen · center

Keep an eye on gravity

Track the black hole’s pull.

Positioned at the bottom center of the screen, the gauge continuously shows the strength of the black hole’s pull. Link orbs, then use a tether to capture or shock one and slightly reduce gravity. Detonating the central bomb can lower it too—a useful way to keep the challenge in check.
